Reimagining Modern Authoring Tools - ATAG 3.0

ATAG CG

Why are we here?

ATAG 2.0 was published over 10 years ago, since then things have changed.

Generative AI, LLMs, and whatever comes next for content production.

ATAG CG is working on the use cases and requirements of a future version of ATAG that considers how authoring has changed.

Current Situation

Content of all kinds can be produced faster than ever before.

Without guardrails, it can also be produced inaccessibly and present risk.

The same tools making content production easier can also make accessible content production easier.

Challenges and Opportunities

  • Remediating older content
  • Detecting or suggesting accessibility features/techniques by content type
  • Addressing specific user needs/wants
  • Assisting content authors in accessible content production
  • Automating accessible content production or repair

Discussion Questions

  • What does an accessibility specification look like for the authoring tools of the present and future?
  • How do we balance the risks and the opportunities of generative AI in authoring contexts
  • What use cases or requirements are we missing?
